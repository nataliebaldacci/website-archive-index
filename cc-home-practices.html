Chiropractic licenses whose address of record is a residence

Licenses whose address of record (DCA license search, 2026-10-26) sits on a parcel the County Assessor codes as residential. A home address of record is allowed (16 CCR 303: the address of record is a mailing address), so this does not mean the chiropractor treats patients there.
